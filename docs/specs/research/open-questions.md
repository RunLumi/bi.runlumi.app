## Hard assumptions to falsify

1. Cross-source discrepancies and inventory/operations decisions are frequent enough that native dashboards + Excel are not adequate.
2. Merchants authorize data and provide a reviewer for money/cost definitions.
3. Source identities and financial coverage support useful verified answers without perpetual manual cleanup.
4. Shared packs reduce implementation/support cost as customers accumulate.
5. Governed AI materially reduces investigation effort after deterministic truth exists.

A failed assumption changes the offer, source scope or architecture. It is not repaired with more polished AI language.

## Product promises not yet earned

No universal real-time data, guaranteed 10% improvement, complete cost-at-sale, full Shopee finance access, perfect autonomous actions, Vietnam-only residency, blanket legal compliance, unlimited history or fixed infrastructure cost. Each requires separate evidence and a specific supported contract.
