# C27 — Delivery sequence, end-state and stop decisions

Spec: C27 | Status: Target delivery contract | Stage: P0–P3

Owns: dependency ordering, investment gates and commercial falsification. This is not a calendar promise or permission to build every end-state capability immediately.

## One coherent end-state

Lumi is the merchant's governed intelligence and decision layer: source access → preserved raw evidence → canonical commerce identities → reconciled metrics → human-readable decisions → optional approved actions → observed outcome. It keeps the existing OMS/marketplace stack and makes reusable commerce definitions, reconciliation knowledge and intervention patterns compound across merchants.

The differentiator is not the number of connectors or an unrestricted SQL chatbot. It is reliable cross-source truth, fast implementation, actionable Vietnamese commerce workflows and evidence that decisions improve.

## Critical path

C01 tenant authority → C02/C03 connection + raw capture → C06 durable publication → C07 source identity + C08 reconciliation → C09/C10 semantic read → C11/C12 Money Truth → C18 merchant review → C26 canary evidence.

C22 security applies from the first commit. C23 infrastructure starts as a bounded implementation, not a distributed platform project. Haravan fixtures validate provider neutrality alongside Nhanh. Shopee authorized-access discovery starts day one because waiting until after UI completion wastes time; lack of access does not justify invented protocol details.

## First 7 days — buy decisive information

Founder plus engineering/data owner obtain three authorized sample sets: Nhanh or Haravan orders, matching marketplace/OMS references, and a closed payout/COD statement/receipt source. Agree what 'sales', 'profit' and 'money received' mean with one merchant steward. Capture overlap IDs, stock-only update behavior, cost history availability and scope restrictions.

Deliver a synthetic end-to-end fixture plus a private merchant reconciliation sheet/record, source access contract, first source coverage page and a bounded three-number query. Verify no A→B access. Do not start with a universal chart canvas or ten connector stubs.

Go: merchant recognizes a recurring costly discrepancy/decision and grants evidence access. Stop/reframe: only interest in generic AI, no source permission, or existing native reports already solve the chosen task at acceptable cost.

## Days 8–30 — paid proof of truth (P0)

Build Nhanh read path, durable raw receipts/outbox, incremental+backfill windows, canonical source mapping, quality checks and publication snapshots. Support authorized settlement/expense exports for missing direct feeds. Ship Money Truth with sales/cost coverage, payout/COD bridge and an investigation queue; current stock visibility only where inventory coverage is certified. Preserve the operations-cost bootstrap as a regression pack, not a competing product direction.

Implement target central identity/registry minimally without granting it all commerce bindings; existing cell authority can transition gradually. Frontend migration follows one useful React/Vite surface rather than a full rewrite before merchant validation.

Gate: one paid merchant, three agreed controls reproducible, one reviewed discrepancy with evidence, explicit unsupported metrics, successful crash/replay and tenant-negative tests. A margin graph does not pass if historical COGS is missing. Engineering records onboarding hours and support burden.

## Days 31–90 — repeatable commerce product (P1)

Certify Haravan; certify direct Shopee only after official access fixtures and financial capability evidence. Prove order dedup between OMS and marketplace. Add movement-backed stock aging where data exists, fulfillment exception workbench, immutable tenant pack publishing/rollback, versioned metric catalog, narrow Ask Lumi over approved questions and role-safe briefs/exports. Add metering and contract/entitlement lifecycle; billing does not weaken authorization.

Gate: a small paid cohort using the same pack, repeat weekly decisions, source reconciliation evidence, third similar onboarding under the targeted engineering budget, measured support cost and renewal intent/payment. At least two source adapters satisfy the same canonical/metric contract with provider-specific semantics retained.

Read/write execution stays separate. Do not allow automated refunds, pricing or ad changes to compensate for weak BI product adoption.

## Months 3–6 — durable intelligence (P2)

Add demand-gated TikTok Shop or another source only if it unlocks a repeated paid decision. Introduce seasonally aware detectors, decomposition investigations, tested forecasts, supplier/inbound planning, scoped partner workspaces and audited read-only external tools. Add a production analytical adapter when measured D1 limits/latency justify it, not because the competitor has a lakehouse.

Promote Ask Lumi from curated questions only when the Vietnamese regression corpus and numerical verifier are reliable. Turn source/metric corrections into reusable fixtures. Introduce L1 internal drafts and a few L2 action handoffs under C21, each with independent policy and outcome verification.

Gate: retained merchants use decisions rather than only opening dashboards; defect recurrence and support time fall; measured unit economics remain positive after humans/model costs; no cross-tenant or privacy regressions.

## Months 6–12 — platform compounding (P2/P3)

Self-service tenant packs, stronger partner deployment, multi-store planning, controlled scenario/experiment workflows, specialized vertical packs and high-volume query paths. Mature central fleet/routing, enterprise identity, recovery and privacy controls based on signed requirements. Expand source coverage along proven economics, not a logo wall.

## Months 12–24+ — end-game options, not a shopping list

A merchant can ask a question, inspect evidence, rehearse a decision, approve a bounded response and measure the result across systems. Repeated decisions can become certified automation packs. Portfolio reporting, constraint optimization, secure data sharing and privacy-reviewed benchmark products require explicit customer demand and data rights. Model vendors and storage engines remain replaceable underneath semantic/evidence contracts.

Do not build in year one: general Spark/lakehouse compute, a notebook platform, unbounded customer SQL hosting, a public plugin marketplace, a new OMS/POS/accounting suite, consumer credit/identity scoring, unrestricted multi-agent swarms or guaranteed fully autonomous commercial operations.

## Team and work allocation

Small-team starting pattern: one source/data engineer; one product/full-stack engineer; founder owns merchant discovery, pricing and source access; a part-time finance/commerce reviewer verifies semantics; targeted security review before paid exposure. These are responsibilities, not an expensive management org chart. If capacity is one engineer, reduce connector breadth rather than removing correctness gates.

Every sprint ends with a merchant-visible verified result or a falsified assumption. Each customer deployment leaves source mapping, quality controls, metric tests, pack version and runbook. The source branch, quality issue and customer case must share evidence IDs.

## Commercial falsification gates

Initial decision heuristics, not predictive guarantees: 20 qualified conversations should generate repeated pain, several authorized data trials and at least two paid pilots. If not, inspect buyer urgency, access friction, native alternatives and offer before building more features. If similar deployments remain mostly bespoke or support consumes the recurring fee, narrow the pack/ICP or change price/scope. If source fees/cost history remain inaccessible, sell a truthful cash/operations product rather than a false profitability promise.

## Contract and acceptance

- **C27-R01:** implementation status lives with code/test evidence, not document completion. Every milestone states shipped, verified, blocked and deferred explicitly.
- **C27-R02:** an uncertain provider capability is tested before dependent commitments; no production timetable assumes access already exists.
- **C27-R03:** feature expansion requires repeated merchant demand and measured reliability/economics. Technical feasibility alone is insufficient.
- **C27-A01:** first pilot traverses source→receipt→canonical→reconciliation→metric→decision, not disconnected demos.
- **C27-A02:** milestone review can reproduce its acceptance evidence and name remaining blockers.
- **C27-A03:** loss of source access or weak economics changes scope visibly rather than silently weakening the product claim.
- **C27-A04:** roadmap work that cannot name a customer decision, canonical owner and test is deferred.

**Highest-leverage next action:** obtain one merchant's overlapping OMS/marketplace orders and a closed settlement period, then reconcile them together before expanding implementation.
