# C00 — Product, market and winning thesis

Spec: C00 | Status: Target contract | Stage: P0–P3

Owns: customer selection, jobs-to-be-done, differentiation and commercial proof. Excludes: formulas (C09), entitlements/prices (C24), implementation sequencing (C27).

## Outcome

An owner/operator can answer: **Which exceptions cost us money or working capital, why do we believe that, and what should happen next?** A finance operator can reproduce the answer without trusting an LLM. A second merchant can adopt the same pack without a new product fork.

Vietnam-first means Vietnamese commercial language, VND, Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh business dates, marketplace/OMS overlap, COD and settlement reality, SKU variants/bundles and low implementation capacity. It does not mean every Vietnamese retailer has the same process.

## Initial customer hypothesis

Start with owner-led merchants using **one of Nhanh or Haravan plus Shopee**, several storefronts or warehouses, and recurring reconciliation/inventory/fulfillment work. A qualifying customer can provide authorized source access or exports, a named operational owner, and one costly recurring decision. Order volume is a qualification signal, not proof of pain. A provisional research band of 3,000–100,000 monthly orders is a sampling hypothesis, not a product limit or market fact.

Users: finance/reconciliation operator, commerce manager, inventory planner, founder. Payer: owner/GM/finance lead. Vetoes: system owner, platform access restrictions, data/privacy objections, unreliable cost data and employees expected to maintain the result. Ask each explicitly.

Disqualify a one-store seller satisfied with native reports; a merchant unwilling to authorize data; or a buyer demanding enterprise-wide automation before a single verified answer. Do not pursue regulated credit scoring, consumer surveillance or enterprise ERP replacement as the first wedge.

## Three decision products

1. **Money Truth:** unify source observations, explain order-to-settlement differences, expose fee/return/cost uncertainty and maintain a resolvable discrepancy queue.
2. **Stock Decisions:** separate physical inventory from channel allocations; identify stockout risk and stock that is not moving; give a reviewable transfer/replenishment proposal.
3. **Operations Exceptions:** identify overdue fulfillment/returns and repetitive coordination; measure actual remaining work after intervention.

Executive Pulse summarizes these products; it is not a fourth independent metric engine. Native channel dashboards remain useful. Lumi must win the cross-system reconciliation, business meaning, evidence and follow-through that a single-source report cannot provide—not claim its charts are inherently better.

## Contract

- **C00-R01:** every pack declares a decision, user, accountable owner, evidence requirements, alternative workflow and observable value. A chart without an associated question is not a roadmap item.
- **C00-R02:** adoption starts read-only; onboarding should end with three merchant-confirmed numbers and one reviewed discrepancy, not merely an OAuth success.
- **C00-R03:** demo data, customer facts, hypotheses, verified results and forecasts are visibly distinct. No invented testimonial, ROI percentage or guaranteed recovered revenue.
- **C00-R04:** every repeated deployment yields mappings, reconciliation fixtures, metrics and a runbook. Custom code belongs in reusable adapters; a fork is an exception requiring an ADR and maintenance budget.
- **C00-R05:** customer interviews inspect the actual report/statement/process. Asking whether they like AI is not demand validation.

## Proof and economics

For each pilot record the existing alternative, time spent, source access effort, discrepancy handling, repeat usage and payment. Track verified decision cycles per merchant, time to first reconciled result, cost to onboard, monthly support hours and percentage of reused definitions. Hours released, cash actually recovered, avoided expense and contribution improvement remain separate outcome classes (C14).

A discrepancy amount is **money to investigate**, not revenue recovered. A stockout forecast is not realized lost sales. A recommendation accepted is not a successful action.

The strongest objection is credible: native reporting plus Excel may already be good enough, while inconsistent data costs more to fix than merchants will pay. Cheapest test: reproduce one monthly settlement/operations workflow for three paying design partners using real authorized samples before building a canvas, universal connector catalog or forecasting platform.

## Acceptance

- **C00-A01:** a merchant can identify the source of a disputed order amount and the responsible next action in a guided test without developer narration.
- **C00-A02:** a customer lacking cost-at-sale sees a useful sales/cash product but no false total profit.
- **C00-A03:** the second merchant's configuration reuses the same pack; adaptations and hours are recorded, not concealed in support work.
- **C00-A04:** a pilot fails the commercial gate when no repeated decision or willingness to pay is observed, even if all dashboards load.

## End-state boundary

P2/P3 add portfolio planning, governed scenario analysis, partner-managed multi-store operations and safe actions. Do not build a new POS, OMS, ad-buying platform, accounting ledger or Databricks clone. Lumi integrates those systems and makes their disagreements explainable.
