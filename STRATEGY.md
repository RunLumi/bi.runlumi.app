# Product direction

Lumi BI optimizes for time to a trusted operational decision and declining setup
cost for repeated installations. Correctness, provenance, access control, and
honest unavailable states outrank chart count or demo polish.

The durable product boundary is a small, self-hosted application with a fixed
resource set, governed metrics, visible evidence, local administration, and
reviewable updates. Do not add a platform registry, universal connector catalog,
generic report language, or marketplace without a measured installation need.
